# 📖 Lesson 3: David — Interactive Presentation

> A beautiful, interactive Bible lesson for kids — **Level 1 & Level 2** — fully based on your document.

## ✨ Features
- 🐑 Both levels from your original lesson plan
- 🎨 Colorful, kid-friendly design
- ⌨️ Keyboard navigation (← → arrow keys)
- 🖨️ Print-friendly activity pages
- 📋 One-click copy for Google Classroom captions
- 📱 Works on phones, tablets, and computers
- 🆓 Hosted free on GitHub Pages

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Go to the **published link** at the top of this repo
2. Click your level:
   - **Level 1** — Simplified, younger learners
   - **Level 2** — Deeper discussion, older learners
3. Use **Next/Previous** buttons or **arrow keys** to move through slides
4. Click discussion questions to reveal answers
5. Use **Print** button to print the craft activity

## 📚 Lesson Content
- **The Big Truth**: God looks at the heart
- **Memory Verse**: Psalm 23:1–2 (L1) / Psalm 23:1–3 (L2)
- **Bible Story**: 1 Samuel 16–17 & Psalm 51
- **Key Events**: Anointing, Goliath, Repentance, God's Faithfulness
- **Activity**: David's Sheep craft with cotton wool

## 🙏 Main Takeaway
> *"We do not need to be perfect — we just need a heart that trusts God, obeys Him, and turns back to Him when we sin."*
